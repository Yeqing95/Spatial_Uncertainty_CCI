3D semi-synthetic generator: scMultiSim background WITHOUT native CCI, plus explicit 3D signaling.
G(i,j,k) = (i != j) and distance(Xi,Xj) <= radius(k). G does not depend on expression.
W(i,j,k) = G * exp(-distance/decay) * L_i/(L_i+K_L) * R_j/(R_j+K_R).
lambda[j,response_k] += response_gain_k * sum_i W(i,j,k).
W is a generating signal, NOT a probability or COMMOT ground-truth score.
An active edge has W > 0. An admissible edge may have W = 0 because of true molecular absence.
Synthetic contact is a centroid-distance proxy. No membrane contact, ECM, protein kinetics, or barriers are modeled.
Radii are controlled simulation parameters, not inferred from LR database membership.
All nonself cell pairs and the listed LRs form the tested universe; absent G entries are structural negatives.
No claims are made about unlisted LR pairs. Genes are synthetic identifiers, not mouse/human symbols.
